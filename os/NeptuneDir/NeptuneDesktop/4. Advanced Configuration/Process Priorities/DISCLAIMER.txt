HIGH PRIORITY DISCLAIMER:

Depending on your hardware, higher process priority can squeeze out extra performance in games, but other times it can cause issues.

Before testing these, I recommend trying high priority through task manager / process lasso.

=========================================================================

LOWER PRIORITY DISCLAIMER:

Setting processes to lower priority doesn't do much, but can help.

Most background processes for applications such as game launchers are already set to "Below Normal" by default.
